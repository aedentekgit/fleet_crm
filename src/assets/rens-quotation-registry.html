About this registry

Data entered here is shared with everyone who opens this tool (it is not private to one device). Customers are registered once, then reused when building a quotation. Each quotation stores its own lorry-type columns and route rates, so different customers can keep different tonnage/length structures — matching how your paper quotations vary by client. Use "View / print" on a saved quotation to get a letterhead-formatted copy.
